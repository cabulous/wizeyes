## Introduction

Welcome to Our Little Kitchen. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information and data. The policy is applicable to the Our Little Kitchen app available on iPhone.

## Information Collection and Use

Our Little Kitchen is designed to store all your data on your device and in your iCloud account. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information through Our Little Kitchen, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Data Storage and Security

Everything you enter into Our Little Kitchen, including recipes, photos, categories, favourites, and the names you give yourself and the members of your kitchen, is stored on your device and synced with your iCloud account. We do not have access to any of this data, and we do not store or transmit it to any external servers.

## Sharing a Kitchen

When you invite someone to your kitchen, Apple's iCloud sharing makes your kitchen's recipes, photos, categories, and favourites available to that person. Only the people you invite can see your kitchen, and as the owner you can change what each person can do or remove them at any time. If you join someone else's kitchen, its data is stored in the owner's iCloud account and shared with you in the same way.

## Camera and Photos

Our Little Kitchen asks for permission to use your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo of a dish. The photos you pick are saved with that recipe in your kitchen and are not used for anything else.

## iCloud

Your data is stored in your iCloud account and is subject to Apple iCloud Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at [support@wizeyes.com](mailto:support@wizeyes.com).
